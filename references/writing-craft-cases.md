# Writing Craft: Conditional Cases

Read only a matching W section when a concrete wording decision is unclear;
do not preload this bank, fetch its videos or add review steps.

Research basis: selected public editing lessons by 周三顾, reviewed 2026-09-29.
C IDs refer to the research cases. Descriptions are editorial paraphrases except
the marked short quotes. Transcripts were read in full; selected wording was
checked on screen, not every word of every video. Literary editing can change
author intent; these skills cannot. Fictional workplace transfers below are our
examples, not the speaker's words or evidence of actual outcomes. No private
media, full transcripts or upload links are included.

## W01 Match The Verb To The Event

**Observed pair C05:** “时常保持年级第一” → “时常考年级第一”. Screen-checked.
A sustained-state verb becomes an event compatible with repeated occurrence.
This works only if intermittent results are the intended meaning. Distinguish
frequency from persistence; do not blacklist a verb.

**Fictional transfer:** source says a service ranked first in three of five
tests. “五次测试中三次排第一” is supported; “始终保持第一” is not. Without source
numbers, inventing five tests or three wins is also wrong.

Source: [改稿60](https://www.douyin.com/video/7684866600268205363), 02:11–03:10.

## W02 Repetition Can Carry A Relationship

**Observed edit C01:** the speaker adds another “一定记得” to connect the narrator
to a remembered image. Screen-checked before/after; repetition serves recognition
and emphasis rather than another fact. Name that role before deleting it.

**Fictional keep example:** “已验证导出权限；未验证导出速度。” Repeating the action
connects different test dimensions. “导出已验证” loses the untested boundary.
Shared technical terms and distinct reading surfaces may repeat; this does not
justify repeating every conclusion.

Source: [改稿59](https://www.douyin.com/video/7680409398241447206), 00:33–01:25.

## W03 A Longer Original May Be Better

**Observed reversal C13, paraphrased:** the speaker shortens a sentence about an
unchanged response after a caretaker changes, then restores the longer version
containing that relation. Both screen states checked. The rejected short trial
is not the original. In C12 an earlier referent justifies a connective; in C24
later speech requires restoring an earlier speech marker (transcript-checked).

**Fictional keep examples:** “入口虽然换了，审批要求仍未改变。” Preserve the unchanged
requirement. “产品同事预计周五完成” cannot become “周五完成”: attribution and
uncertainty disappear. Inspect dependencies on both sides before cutting.

Sources: [改稿61](https://www.douyin.com/video/7690460905473101102), 30:40–30:58,
1:32:20–1:34:10; [编辑改稿](https://www.douyin.com/video/7673732283232794853),
05:33–06:10.

## W04 Tiny Words Change Stance

**Observed contrast C14:** removing “个” in “美得不像个人” can make praise sound
like an insult. Retained version screen-checked; rejected contrast spoken.
**Observed pair C22:** “心下就坦然了” → “心下竟坦然了” adds unexpectedness, not just
rhythm. The screen confirms “竟” where transcription misrecognized it. Do not
change expectedness unless author intent or context supports it.

**Fictional keep example:** “结果还没出来，我有点担心。” Adding “竟然” or “终于”
can introduce blame or expectations. Do not erase useful emotion or soften a
real refusal into apparent agreement. These are contextual effects, not bans.

Sources: [改稿61](https://www.douyin.com/video/7690460905473101102),
1:13:37–1:14:20; [文笔诊断](https://www.douyin.com/video/7222161276229782788),
30:49–32:30.

## W05 Sound Does Not Authorize A Quantity Change

**Observed C08/C15, paraphrased:** a stated two-hour duration becomes more than
two hours for cadence; an approximate childhood age becomes an unqualified age
for a cleaner opening. Transcript-checked. These are migration counterexamples,
not recommended work rewrites. Degree and confidence also carry meaning.

**Fictional reject example:** “大约两小时，可能还要复核” → “两小时内完成” adds a
deadline and certainty. Preserve the estimate and possible review.

Sources: [改稿57](https://www.douyin.com/video/7673778083719073024), 00:00–01:10;
[改稿61](https://www.douyin.com/video/7690460905473101102), 00:00–01:09.

## W06 Give Details A Job

**Observed C03/C17, paraphrased:** a nickname explanation drifts into sports,
appearance and biography; another edit removes a trailing image to leave the
focus on an ongoing action. Transcript-checked diagnoses, not full quote pairs.
Information may belong elsewhere rather than be useless.

**Fictional transfer:** a launch-blocker note first explains the missing approval
and its effect. Supported interface history may move to background, while a
security condition stays beside the launch judgment. Do not force every
paragraph into a conclusion/evidence/action template or remove needed reasons.

Sources: [改稿59](https://www.douyin.com/video/7680409398241447206), 05:36–07:12;
[改稿61](https://www.douyin.com/video/7690460905473101102), 13:32–15:33.

## W07 Evaluate Rhythm In Context

**Observed C04/C18, paraphrased:** age moves next to the experience it qualifies;
a locally pleasant pause is rejected because it slows the whole passage.
C04 screen-checked; C18 transcript-based, not a verbatim punctuation pair.
Emphasis and pauses serve the paragraph's task, not a numeric recipe.

**Fictional transfer:** put “尚未开放” before implementation history, then retain
both required release approvals next to the planned opening. Do not drop an
approval to make a punchier line. Business conclusions need no suspense.

Sources: [改稿60](https://www.douyin.com/video/7684866600268205363), 00:00–02:20;
[改稿61](https://www.douyin.com/video/7690460905473101102), 38:52–41:15.
The spring-window example also appears in the longer lesson: one manuscript,
not independent evidence.

## W08 Plausibility Is Not Source Support

**Observed C09, paraphrased:** a one-handed action is supplied to make a cycling
scene plausible. Transcript-checked. Diagnosing inconsistency is useful; the
missing action remains unconfirmed. Describe only supported actions or ask.

**Fictional reject example:** “用户未完成支付” does not support “用户因价格过高放弃
支付”. A missing reply does not prove neglect or a hidden blocker. More specific
can be less truthful; do not invent motives or mechanics for vividness.

Source: [改稿57](https://www.douyin.com/video/7673778083719073024), 02:46–04:35.

## Use Boundary

Do not turn personal repetition windows, parallel-clause counts, tense
preferences or permission to change intent into gates. Cases explain decisions,
not sentences to imitate. No new score or reviewer: confirmed reader harm uses
the existing revision, meaning comparison and retry limits. Source links are
provenance, not retrieval steps for routine use.
