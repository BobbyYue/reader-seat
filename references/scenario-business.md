# Business Update, Review, And Retrospective

## Reader Task

Enable stakeholders to judge progress and impact, understand material variance and risk, and make any required decision or provide support.

## Required Content

- objective, baseline, expected result, and evaluation period;
- actual result and business or user impact;
- key progress only when it explains the result;
- material variance, drivers, constraints, and credible alternatives;
- current risks, dependencies, and what is already being handled;
- decision, support, action, owner, and timing when applicable;
- learning and changed practice for retrospectives or performance summaries.

## Recommended Relationship

`objective and actual result -> business impact -> material progress or variance drivers -> risks and dependencies -> decision, request, owner, and timing`

Use comparison tables for planned versus actual or prior versus current. Separate outcomes from effort. Put project history in an appendix when it does not change the decision.

## Evidence Requirements

- Include the comparison basis, period, units, denominator, and scope for key results.
- Tie activities to observable outcomes; label expected future impact as a forecast.
- Distinguish external constraints from controllable execution issues without shifting unsupported blame.
- Do not convert an individual contribution into team or business impact without evidence.

## Common Failures

- activity volume presented as achievement;
- positive result with no baseline or business meaning;
- every detail treated as equally important;
- problem stated without owner, decision, or recovery path;
- request hidden at the end or phrased as vague “support needed”;
- retrospective lists events but changes no future practice.

## Acceptance Questions

1. Can the reader compare objective, expected result, and actual result?
2. Can they identify the business or user impact?
3. Are the main drivers and alternative explanations clear?
4. Can they identify the highest-priority risk or decision?
5. When action is needed, are owner, timing, dependency, and ask complete?
