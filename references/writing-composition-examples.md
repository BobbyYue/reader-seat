# Whole-Piece Choices, Not Sentence Decoration

Read only the relevant example when material selection or ordering is unresolved.
These are fictional teaching examples, not verified business results, quotes
from Zhou Sangu, genre templates, or factual sources for a user's document.
They complement the video-derived W cases; they are not attributed to that author.
Use a user's supplied complete exemplar when it better matches the task, without
persisting private text in this bank. Learn what was chosen, omitted, connected
and left uncertain; do not copy the voice, facts or visible structure.

## C01 Reader's Seat: Explain The Decision, Not The Work Log

**Reader/task:** a product colleague deciding whether to expand a pilot.
**Available material:** one FAQ pilot ran for a week; 20 selected questions,
16 answered correctly, 4 failed because documents were outdated; no access
control test, maintenance-cost measure or expansion decision. A team meeting
and a renamed spreadsheet do not change this decision.

**Weak complete passage:**
> 本周完成了会议讨论、表格整理和知识库问答测试，各项工作有序推进。
> 测试取得积极进展，后续将持续优化体验，助力知识服务升级。

**Reader-fit complete passage:**
> 这次测试说明，已有文档能支撑一部分常见问答，但还不足以判断能否扩大使用。
> 选取的 20 个问题中，16 个答对；另外 4 个因文档过期答错。下一步的难点不只是怎么回答，还包括谁来维护答案依据。
> 权限控制和维护成本尚未测试。这一周的结果不能代表更多问题或更多使用者的表现。

**Why:** the opening gives a bounded judgment, evidence explains it, and the
ending identifies the actual limit. The maintenance concern is an interpretation
of stated failures, not a claim of measured cost or an assigned commitment.
Administrative details are omitted because they do not change this reader's
decision. For an implementation handoff, those details may matter.

**Transfer:** use the required-answer plan, not this paragraph order. A news
update can lead with what changed; a procedure can lead with the next operation.
Do not invent a scene or add a proposed rollout simply to give the piece an ending.

## C02 3080: Select A Relationship Before Compressing

**Reader/task:** choose between two tested settings.
**Source:** same test environment; A completes 120 jobs/minute with 3 failures
per 100 jobs, B completes 90 with 1 per 100; no priority between speed and errors.
No online test. All numbers are fictional.

**Weak judgment:**
> A 效率领先，B 稳定可靠，两者各有优势，可按需选择。

**Reader-fit miniature:**
> 当前测试中，A 更快、失败也更多；原文没有给出速度与失败率的取舍标准，暂不能判定哪种更合适。
>
> A：120 个/分钟，失败率 3%；B：90 个/分钟，失败率 1%。
>
> 要做选择，还缺什么？需要取舍标准；线上表现也尚未验证。

**Why:** remove unsupported reliability language and surface the real choice.
Do not turn this miniature into a complete 3080 artifact: the mandatory visual,
question table, all P0 claims and coverage requirements still apply. Map the
same tradeoff into the existing visual; use questions for missing decision
criteria, not a third copy of the takeaway. Keep environment and units adjacent.

**Transfer:** a factual summary may have no contrast. Do not manufacture a
winner, opposing options, story, analogy or recommendation absent from the source.

## C03 Hit Send: Preserve The Person's Position

**Context:** agreed review was today; an external reply is needed tomorrow;
the draft is still missing. The writer is worried, not cancelling the review.

**Weak polished message:**
> 温馨提醒，请您百忙之中关注材料进度，感谢您的理解与支持。

**Reader-fit complete message:**
> 说好今天过一遍，我这边还没收到稿子。明天要对外回复，我有点担心来不及。你看今天能发我吗？

**Why:** the source agreement and practical concern explain the request without
guessing why the recipient is late. The last sentence asks whether today is
possible; it does not announce a new promise. Conversational words carry the
writer's concern and should not be removed merely to shorten the message.

**Already clear:**
> 收到，我周四下午三点前把修改稿发你。

Keep exactly as supplied if these are the user's facts. Do not invent a fuller
background, warmer ending, alternative version or extra ask.

## Compare Within The Existing Review

For the actual candidate, explain which reader question is now easier to answer,
which relationship or boundary became clearer, or which unnecessary reply was
avoided. Do not claim measured time saved or a real reader-test result without
evidence. A plausible editorial judgment and an observed reader result differ.
If the old version works as well, retain it. Literary flavor is not a release
condition; use existing review limits and no additional style-only loop.
