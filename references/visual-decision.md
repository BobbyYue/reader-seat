# Visual Decision Gate

Use this short module for every finished artifact before deciding whether to
use charts, diagrams, tables, screenshots, callouts, cards, semantic color, or
layout that visually encodes meaning. Its purpose is to make the no-visual
decision cheap without removing any visual capability.

If the decision is to retain, create, or materially revise a visual form, load
and follow [visual-communication.md](visual-communication.md) before designing
it. If the form is a photo, illustration, screenshot, chart, diagram, or other
asset with provenance implications, also load
[visual-evidence.md](visual-evidence.md).

The governing outcome is:

`the reader reaches the correct judgment with less effort`

Visuals are not a quota, decoration, or proof of polish. Use one only when it
performs a reader task more efficiently or accurately than the best concise
prose alternative.

## Valid Reader Jobs

Every retained visual element must perform at least one explicit job:

1. **Locate**: make status, priority, ownership, exception, or the next action
   easy to find.
2. **Compare**: reveal differences in magnitude, composition, ranking, trend,
   or alternatives.
3. **Explain**: make a sequence, system, dependency, mechanism, or hierarchy
   easier to understand.
4. **Emphasize**: direct attention to one evidence-supported item without
   changing its apparent importance.
5. **Verify**: expose values, definitions, sources, uncertainty, or a real
   product state so the reader can check a claim.

`Make the page attractive`, `balance the layout`, and `every section needs a
visual` are not valid jobs.

## Mandatory Decision

Before selecting a visual form:

1. State the reader question the element must answer.
2. State the source evidence available to answer it.
3. Write the shortest accurate prose alternative.
4. Select a visual only when spatial encoding materially improves retrieval,
   comparison, explanation, emphasis, or verification.
5. Apply the deletion test: if removing the proposed element would not make the
   target reader slower, less accurate, or less able to verify the answer,
   omit it.
6. Reject any form that implies a relationship, precision, ranking, magnitude,
   completeness, or causality unsupported by the source.

When the source is too thin, use bounded prose or a clearly labeled unknown.
Do not fill a visual with assumptions.

## Decision States

Record one state internally:

- `no-visual`: concise prose is at least as effective; do not load the full
  visual implementation module.
- `retain-visual`: a chart, diagram, table, screenshot, callout, semantic color,
  card group, or encoded layout has a valid reader job; load
  [visual-communication.md](visual-communication.md).
- `asset-visual`: the retained form also makes a provenance, identity,
  authenticity, licensing-context, or synthetic-status claim; load both
  [visual-communication.md](visual-communication.md) and
  [visual-evidence.md](visual-evidence.md).

Two to four short peer conclusions with no shared comparison fields remain a
list or unframed rows. Do not turn them into one card each merely to make the
page look richer. Baseline typography, spacing, and hierarchy are presentation,
not a reason to retain a material visual.

## Exit Check

The decision is complete only when the reader question, prose alternative,
selected state, and deletion result are recorded. A retained visual may not be
built until the full implementation module is loaded.
